Accessibility

Clear thinking should
be easier to access.

We aim to make this website usable across keyboards, touch devices, screen readers, zoomed layouts and reduced-motion settings.

Our target

We are designing toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA. That includes visible keyboard focus, meaningful page structure, sufficient target sizes, responsive reflow, text alternatives where needed, reduced-motion support and interactions that do not depend on a mouse.

Interactive content

The diagnostic tool and interactive homepage use pointer and keyboard controls. Touch instructions are shown on touch devices. Where a gesture is available, a tap or keyboard alternative should also be available.

Known limitations

This website is still in pre-launch QA. We are testing responsive layouts, colour contrast, keyboard access and assistive-technology behaviour. A remaining issue should be treated as a design defect, not as something the visitor is expected to work around.

Tell us about a barrier

If something is difficult to use, please email problemstudio@lillithindustries.com. Tell us the page, device or assistive technology you were using and what you were trying to do.
