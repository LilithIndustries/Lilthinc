How we work

Get close enough
to see what is true.

We start with the problem you can see, then test what is actually producing it.

The aim is simple: give you a clear view of the problem, the evidence behind it, and a practical way forward.

The principle

Inside the information.
Outside the hierarchy.

We stay external so we can look across teams, incentives, systems and history without being absorbed into one internal view. We listen to the people closest to the work and test what we hear against evidence.

We do not arrive with a preferred answer. We build more than one explanation and try to disprove our own thinking before recommending a change.

What happens
01

Understand what is happening

We clarify what is known, what is assumed, what is disputed and what is still unclear.

→
02

See the work as it actually happens

We speak with the people involved, review evidence and trace real examples from start to finish.

→
03

Look across the gaps between teams

We check handoffs, decisions, incentives, information and workarounds — especially where one part of the system meets another.

→
04

Test more than one explanation

We keep competing possibilities alive until the evidence narrows them.

→
05

Stress-test the response

We check what the proposed change could improve, what it could make worse and what it assumes.

→
06

Give you a clear way forward

You receive the evidence, the problem as we understand it, the options and a recommended sequence. Your team owns the decision.

→
Human systems

People are part of the operating system.

Whether people feel safe enough to raise a problem, clear enough to decide, or overloaded enough to stop noticing risk affects how the business operates.

We look at observable effects — silence, workarounds, delay, rework, escalation and decision behaviour. We do not diagnose personalities.

The engagement

One important problem.
A focused investigation.

The exact scope depends on the problem, the evidence available and who needs to be involved. We agree that scope before work begins.

Pricing is discussed privately after the initial problem conversation.
