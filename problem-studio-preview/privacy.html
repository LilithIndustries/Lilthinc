Privacy

What we collect.
Why we collect it.

Draft for preview. This policy must be checked against the final CRM, analytics, email, hosting and data-retention setup before production launch.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, work email address, company, role and the information you include in an enquiry. We may also collect basic technical and usage information needed to operate and understand the website.

2. Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess whether we may be a fit for an engagement, provide requested services, operate and secure the website, and improve how the site works.

3. Sensitive and confidential information

Please avoid including sensitive personal information, detailed employee information, legally privileged material or confidential commercial information in the initial enquiry form. We will arrange an appropriate channel before receiving detailed client material.

4. Service providers

We may use service providers for hosting, customer relationship management, email, scheduling, analytics and security. The final production list and any overseas processing locations will be confirmed before launch.

5. Access, correction and questions

You may contact us to ask about personal information we hold about you, request correction where appropriate, or raise a privacy concern. Contact details will be inserted before production launch.

6. Updates

We will update this policy when our information-handling practices change.
