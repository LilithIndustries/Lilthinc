How we judge it

Capability is not
the same as suitability.

“Can AI do this?” and “Could AI do this in my business?” are different questions.

Can

Technical capability: can current AI perform meaningful parts of the task?

Could

Practical fit: is this commonly workable once access, context, controls and authority are considered?

Boundary

What still needs a person, approval, accountability or real-world action?

Readiness

AI-ready, AI-adjacent, human-led, human-required, or simply not worth automating.
