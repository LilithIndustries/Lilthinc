Cookies & privacy choices

Tracking should be
clear and optional.

This page explains the browser storage and tracking technologies used on this website.

Current status

The current preview does not load third-party analytics or advertising trackers. Essential browser functions may still use local storage or similar technology where needed for site operation or to remember a privacy choice.

Essential storage

Essential storage supports functions a visitor requests or that are necessary to operate the website securely. It is not used to build advertising profiles.

Optional analytics

If analytics is added, it will be treated as optional and will remain off until consent is recorded where consent is required. Analytics should be configured to collect the minimum information needed to understand site performance.

Marketing and advertising

Advertising pixels or cross-site marketing trackers are not active on the current preview. If they are added later, they will be separately disclosed and gated behind the relevant privacy choice.

Changing your choice

When optional tracking is active, a privacy-choice control will let you change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawing consent will stop future optional tracking; it cannot erase information already lawfully processed by a third-party provider.

Questions
